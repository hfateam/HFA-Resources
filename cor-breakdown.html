Start with what doesn't wait on your COR

Your COR (Custodian of Records background clearance) is usually the slowest part of getting approved. So the smartest way to begin is to knock out everything that doesn't depend on it first, and circle back to the rest once your clearance is in. This tool sorts every school by exactly that.

Start now You can apply before COR is finalized — or there's no background check at all (the ESAs). After COR Get your background check sorted first, then apply. Closed Not taking vendors right now.

Two reminders: family-request schools still need a family to get things started, even when they're marked "Start now." And this is a starting point — use your own judgment for your niche, and reach out with questions.
