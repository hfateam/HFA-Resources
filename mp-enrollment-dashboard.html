Methodology. Semesters are grouped into academic years as Fall + the following Spring (Fall 2023 had no paired spring session on record). "2026–27" reflects only Fall 2026 to date and is not comparable to a complete year. School names have been consolidated where the source data used more than one spelling for the same school (e.g. "Sky Mountain" / "Sky Mountain Charter"). Records with no school on file, along with entries that name a payment or funding platform rather than a school (ClassWallet, Venmo, ESA/EFA programs, and similar), are grouped as "Other."

This report contains enrollment counts only. Student names, contact information, ages, class selections, and payment amounts have been excluded.
