Glossary of Key Terms

Homeschool Funding Accelerator — every abbreviation, form name, platform, and program term used across the classroom, in one place.
How to use this: Terms are alphabetical. Each entry names the lesson it's covered in (course › lesson), the same way a textbook index points you to a page. If you hit an unfamiliar term anywhere in the program, look it up here first — if you need the full explanation, the lesson tag tells you exactly where to go. This version leans generous: if there was any chance a term might trip someone up, it's in here, even if it seemed obvious to us.
Watch out — one term means two different things: "COI" shows up twice in the program with two unrelated meanings: a Certificate of Insurance (proof of insurance some schools want) and an ORI/COI number (a DOJ background-check identifier). They're listed separately below so it's clear which is which.

A

Accreditation

Formal recognition of a credential or program. Alabama's ESA accepts proof of accreditation as one option (alongside a degree or teaching certification) to qualify for the Private Tutoring category.

ESA Tutorials › Alabama ESA

Articles of Incorporation / Organization

The legal formation document for your business (filed when you set up your LLC or corporation). Some ESA applications accept this as one acceptable proof of business, in place of a business license.

ESA Tutorials › Utah Fits All, Wyoming
B

BAM (Be a Mentor)

Visions Charter School's specific background check program, used alongside their standard application.

Charter School Tutorials › Visions, Cottonwood, Harvest Ridge

BCIA 9001

A DOJ form in the COR application where you describe the services your agency provides — this is where your own company blurb goes.

HFA Main Course › COR | Becoming a Custodian of Records

BCIA 9017

A DOJ form asking for a brief description of your intended purpose for receiving CORI (criminal history information).

HFA Main Course › COR | Becoming a Custodian of Records

Bulk Upload

The ability to add many classes to a portal at once (usually via spreadsheet/CSV) instead of entering each one by hand. Not every portal offers this — West Virginia's Hope Scholarship, for example, requires classes to be added individually.

ESA Tutorials › West Virginia ESA Application
C

CalVECHS User Agreement

A specific section of the COR application that applies when you select "Youth Org" as your agency type.

HFA Main Course › COR | Becoming a Custodian of Records

ClassWallet

The vendor/payment portal used by several ESA programs (Arizona, Arkansas, New York, Missouri, and others) for vendor registration and getting paid.

HFA Main Course › How Students Pay · ESA Tutorials › Arizona, Alabama · Optimizing Your Listing › ClassWallet | Optimize

ClassWallet Marketplace

An optional add-on (roughly $24/month) that lets families buy directly through ClassWallet like an online store. You do not need this to be listed or paid — it's worth it once you have consistent volume.

ESA Tutorials › Alabama ESA

COI — Certificate of Insurance

Proof-of-insurance paperwork that some schools (like Suncoast) require you to upload with your application.

Charter School Tutorials › Suncoast
Not the same as ORI/COI number below — see the callout above.

COR — Custodian of Records

A California DOJ authorization you need before most CA charter schools will accept you as a vendor. Being a Custodian of Records lets you request criminal background checks on staff. Requires an EIN first.

HFA Main Course › COR | Becoming a Custodian of Records

CORI

Criminal Offender Record Information — the background-check data your COR authorization allows you to request.

HFA Main Course › COR | Becoming a Custodian of Records

CPA — Certified Public Accountant

A licensed tax/accounting professional. Referenced as who to check with on business license and city tax questions, since requirements vary by location.

HFA Main Course › Business License / City Tax

CSF — Children's Scholarship Fund

The organization administering New Hampshire's ESA program.

ESA Tutorials › New Hampshire ESA | Children's Scholarship Fund
D

DBA — Doing Business As

A display or "trade" name your business operates under, which can be different from your official LLC name. Some applications ask for both.

ESA Tutorials › Utah Fits All Scholarship Program

Direct Pay Vendor

A vendor who gets paid directly through the ESA system without using the optional Marketplace add-on. This is the default and is enough to receive payments and appear in the directory.

ESA Tutorials › Alabama ESA

DLS — Detailed List of Services

Your master document describing your classes and pricing. Build it once and most schools will accept it as-is, so you're not rewriting it for every application.

HFA Main Course › Creating Your (DLS) Detailed List of Services

DOJ — Department of Justice

The California agency that issues COR approval and processes fingerprint background checks.

HFA Main Course › COR lessons

Drip Content

A feature on some course-hosting platforms that releases lessons to students gradually over time instead of all at once. Mentioned as available on Skool's cheaper plan, though not recommended due to missing safety features.

HFA Main Course › Part 1: Hosting Pre-Recorded Classes
E

EFA — Education Freedom Account

Arkansas's name for its ESA program.

ESA Tutorials › Arkansas Education Freedom (EFA) Account

EIN — Employer Identification Number

Your business's IRS tax ID number. One of the first things to get — needed before COR and most school applications. Usually issued instantly.

HFA Main Course › Applying for an EIN

ESA — Education Savings Account

A state program that gives families funds to spend on approved vendors like you, instead of (or alongside) public school.

Covered throughout ESA Application Tutorials course
F

FD-258

The physical ink fingerprint card format required for your COR background check. California's digital Live Scan system won't work here — you need the physical hard cards.

HFA Main Course › COR | Preliminary Approval & Fingerprints

Flex Class

A pre-recorded class you list and advertise before you've fully built it out, so you only build content you know there's real demand for.

HFA Main Course › Pre-Recorded Class Creation Tips · Examples of My Flex Classes

Funnel Gorgeous

A website/course-hosting platform (roughly $99–$129/month) offering websites, funnels, course hosting, email marketing, and CRM in one place. One option for hosting pre-recorded classes.

HFA Main Course › Part 1: Hosting Pre-Recorded Classes
G

Google Business Profile

A free business listing on Google (shows up in search and maps) where families can leave reviews. Charter school coordinators and ESA families often check this before approving a vendor.

HFA Main Course › Build Your Google Reviews

Google Classroom

Google's free classroom/learning management tool. A common (free) starting option for hosting pre-recorded classes before upgrading to a paid platform.

HFA Main Course › Part 1 & Part 2: Hosting Pre-Recorded Classes
I

IEM

The umbrella organization that owns three separate California charters — Ocean Grove, South Sutter, and Sky Mountain. Apply once and you're approved for all three.

Charter School Tutorials › IEM: South Sutter, Ocean Grove, Sky Mountain

IEPro (Independent Educator Pro)

Another paid course/business platform option some vendors use, mentioned as an alternative to Google Classroom or Kajabi.

HFA Main Course › Part 1: Hosting Pre-Recorded Classes
K

Kajabi

A premium course-hosting platform (roughly $143–$179/month). One option for hosting pre-recorded classes, though not cheap.

HFA Main Course › Part 1: Hosting Pre-Recorded Classes

Keyword Searchability

How easily families find you when they search a term (like "writing" or "art") inside a vendor directory. Some portals support this well; others barely have it, so it's worth testing rather than assuming.

Optimizing Your Listing › Utah ESA Children's First | Optimize
L

Live Scan

California's digital fingerprinting system — not the same as, and not compatible with, the physical FD-258 cards the DOJ requires for COR.

HFA Main Course › COR | Preliminary Approval & Fingerprints

LLC — Limited Liability Company

A common legal business structure. Forming your LLC is a prerequisite that comes before getting your EIN.

HFA Main Course › Applying for an EIN
M

Micro School

A specific legal school designation — this is not what HFA vendors are (you're a Service Provider, see below).

ESA Tutorials › West Virginia ESA Application
N

Niche Down

Specializing in one subject rather than offering several. Whether this helps you get noticed depends on whether you're relying on a directory search or driving your own traffic to a website.

Bonus Content › Will I Show Up Higher if I Niche Down or Not?

Non-qualifying Expenses

Things an ESA program will not reimburse or fund. Worth checking per state so you don't build an offer families can't actually pay for with their funds.

Optimizing Your Listing › WV ESA | Hope Scholarship | Optimize

Nonpublic School / Participating School

A legal designation for brick-and-mortar private schools — not the category online class vendors fall under. If asked, you're a Service Provider instead.

ESA Tutorials › West Virginia ESA Application
O

Odyssey

The vendor portal shared by Utah Fits All, LA GATOR, and Wyoming ESA. Same process, but you still have to optimize your listing separately for each one.

ESA Tutorials › Utah Fits All, LA GATOR, Wyoming · Optimizing Your Listing › Odyssey (UT, LA, WY)

ORI/COI number

An identifier issued after your DOJ fingerprint processing completes, which charter schools may ask you for as proof of clearance.

HFA Main Course › COR | Preliminary Approval & Fingerprints
Not the same as Certificate of Insurance (COI) above — see the callout at the top of this page.

Outschool

A separate, well-known online marketplace for kids' classes (not a charter school or ESA program). Referenced in the program as a source for pricing comparisons, video examples, and as a place with its own referral/review policies to be aware of.

HFA Main Course › How to do Market Research with AI or DIY · Build Your Google Reviews · Bonus Content › Pricing Comparison to Outschool
P

Participating School

See Nonpublic School / Participating School under N.

ESA Tutorials › West Virginia ESA Application

Passive Student Growth

Getting new students with minimal ongoing time investment, as opposed to actively marketing every week. Framed as an overview concept, with specific application steps to come in later lessons.

HFA Main Course › Passive Student Growth, What Actually Works?

PO — Purchase Order

What a family submits through their school to request funding for your class. Most schools pay after the PO is issued and the class is completed, not upfront.

HFA Main Course › How Students Pay

Pre-Screening

An initial approval step some schools (like Suncoast) require before you move on to the full vendor application.

Charter School Tutorials › Suncoast

Provider Portal

General term for whatever online system a given state uses to manage your ESA vendor application (Odyssey, ClassWallet, and each state's own portal all fall under this).

Covered throughout ESA Application Tutorials course

Provider Type

A category you select during an application (for example, "Educational Services") that tells the portal what kind of vendor you are.

ESA Tutorials › Utah Fits All Scholarship Program
R

Referral-only

Compass Charter's vendor model — a student or family has to request you as a vendor before you're able to apply. You can't apply cold.

Charter School Tutorials › Compass · Family-Requested Schools

Refund Policy

A written policy stating how (or whether) you'll refund families, required as a document for several ESA applications.

ESA Tutorials › Arkansas Education Freedom (EFA) Account · West Virginia ESA Application

Registration Form

The form where you collect key info from families — either before they enroll or right after — so you can give students access to class and keep everything organized.

HFA Main Course › Creating Your Registration Form
S

Semester vs. Monthly Classes

Two common ways to structure how long a class runs and how often families are billed. Which one makes sense depends on your goals and school payment timelines.

HFA Main Course › Scheduling Your Live Classes

Service Provider

The vendor classification that applies to online class businesses like yours, as opposed to "Participating School" (a legal designation for brick-and-mortar schools). If an application asks which one you are, this is you.

ESA Tutorials › West Virginia ESA Application
T

Thinkific

A course-hosting platform (roughly $49–$99/month) offered as a mid-range alternative to Google Classroom or Kajabi.

HFA Main Course › Part 1: Hosting Pre-Recorded Classes

Two-Lesson Buffer

Staying built out about two lessons ahead of where students currently are in a Flex Class, rather than building the entire class in advance.

HFA Main Course › Pre-Recorded Class Creation Tips
U

URL Converter

A tool used to convert a Google Drive image link into a format Odyssey's CSV upload can actually read.

Optimizing Your Listing › Odyssey (UT, LA, WY) | Optimize
V

Vendor Blurb / Vendor Description

Your public-facing description in vendor directories — the thing families read to decide if you're a good fit and that helps you show up in search.

HFA Main Course › Your Vendor Description / Blurb

Vendor Categories

Tags or classifications you select in a directory (like "Online Education Classes" or "Private Tutoring") that determine where and how families can find you when filtering.

ESA Tutorials › Alabama ESA, Arkansas Education Freedom (EFA) Account

Vendor Directory / School Directory

The searchable listing where families find and choose vendors. Same thing — different schools just call it different names.

HFA Main Course › (Draft) School Directory Overview · Optimizing Your Listing › Intro lesson
W

W-9

The IRS tax form several ESA portals require (Arizona ClassWallet, Utah Children's First) to verify your business and set up payment.

ESA Tutorials › Arizona, Utah Children's First
Y

Youth Org

An agency type you select during the COR application if you work with minors. Selecting it triggers additional forms, including the CalVECHS User Agreement.
