What Actually Affects Your Ranking

The real factors to check on your class listings for charter schools and ESA families

What To Check
1

Alphabetical

Where your business name falls alphabetically can play a role in where you show up.

2

Keywords in your title and description

Use the words families are actually searching for, worked naturally into both fields.

3

Tags with keywords

If the platform gives you a tags field, use it. Example: a location-based tag like "San Jose."

Note: it's not confirmed whether this is against platform rules or not — but Meg has tagged the city where the school is located rather than her own city, since she only teaches online, and hasn't run into any issues doing it that way so far.

4

Location

Make sure this field is filled in and accurate — it's one of the places keywords get picked up.

5

Title

Keep it clear and keyword-rich.

6

Photo

Every listing should have one.

7

Fully filled-out listing

Complete every field on your listing — incomplete listings tend to underperform.

8

Compare to competitors

Look at what's working — in your niche, and beyond. See what words show up in their titles, how they describe the class, and what makes their listing immediately understandable. You're not copying — you're identifying patterns families already respond to.

Exact Match

Sometimes a parent has to type your exact business name to find you.

Keyword Match

Other times, a single keyword is enough for your listing to show up.

Check These Fields for Keywords

Keywords can count whether they're in the title, the description, the location, or any other field on the listing — worth checking each one.

  • Title
  • Description
  • Location
  • Tags (if the platform has them)
  • Any other visible field
